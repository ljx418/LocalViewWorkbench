### 10.1 (D1) — the barrier: the wait may not precede its cause

`session.start()`" of §3.6 — the barrier of the seam runs only when the
coordination cancels the read, which `bind_deadline` + `clock.advance`
(`T10:4139-4140`) cause **after** the worker and the generation are
registered (`T10:4125-4134`). The original trigger is preserved, never moved:

| step | action | where |
|---|---|---|
| S1 | `session.start()` | `T10:4119` |
| S2 | bounded wait 15 s for `api.read_waiting` | `T10:4123` |
| S3 | `worker = holder['read_endpoint']._reader`; `api.worker_holder['worker'] = worker` | `T10:4125-4127` |
| S4 | `generation = worker._submitted`; `api.worker_holder['generation'] = generation` | `T10:4128-4134` |
| S5 | `bind_deadline(clock.value + 0.5)`; `clock.advance(1.0)` → the coordination cancels the read → the seam's `cancel_synchronous_io` runs | `T10:4139-4140` |

  barrier block; no "entered" event, no wait before S5.

### 10.2 (D2) — the construction, the phase result, the whole-test guard

**(a) The construction is inside the local guard**, with the holders
initialised to `None` before it: `ledger.attempt(phase + '-construction')`,
then `session = holder = channel_closes = channel_facts = None`, then `try:`
→ `_chain(api, document, plan, tmp_path, clock=clock)` → the phase body →
`except BaseException:` (the FAIL rewrite of §10.6) → `raise` → `finally:`
(the bounded teardown; a `None` holder is skipped, never dereferenced). A
construction that failed half way is therefore an **attempted construction**
with a durable FAIL document, not an absent document.

**(b) The complete inventory** — five names per schedule: `<phase>` (the
**result**), `<phase>-construction`, `<phase>-barrier`, `<phase>-first`,
`<phase>-second`; plus `r10-published-before-timeout{,-first,-second}` and
the earlier R10 documents `r10-first`, `r10-second`, `r10-coordination` →
**31 names**, all `plan`ned at the top of the test.

**(c) The result is separate from the checks.** A passed check is terminal.
The result document is written **last**, after both stops **and** the cleanup,
and becomes PASSED only when the barrier check is PASSED (or NOT_APPLICABLE),
both stop checks are PASSED and the cleanup had no proof failure. A later
failed stop ⇒ the result is **FAIL** while the earlier checks **keep** PASSED
and their facts. The result is never PASSED before the stops and the cleanup.

**(d) The whole-test guard** covers the earlier R10 real-chain work
(`T10:4745-4816`), the coordination block (`T10:4817-4888`) **and** the five
later schedules; the inventory and the ledger are installed before all of it;
the `finally` writes `NOT_RUN` only for the names still PLANNED and never
overwrites a written document.

### 10.3 (D3) — the scope, the exact thread objects, the bounded drain

**(a) The scope** — one registry per round lifetime; the seam is armed for
the whole lifetime and disarmed only after that round's salvage: the R07 main
round (`parent_registry`, armed before `start_round(...)` at `T10:2371`,
disarmed after the parent salvage at `T10:2983-2995`); the zero round
(`zero_registry`, `T10:2682` → `T10:2786-2789`); each controlled subcase
(`controlled_registry`, `T10:3221` → `T10:3370-3374`). The seam stays armed
through the product's `stop()` and the late closer work, so a thread started
during the stop is registered too.

**(b) The exact object, not the name** — the registration is keyed by
`id(thread)`; the name is only a label. The seam forwards every start it
does not own (`if id(thread) not in armed_threads and thread.name not in
owned_names: return original_start(thread, *args, **kwargs)`); the ownership
proof always works on the object, and a name match alone never claims
ownership.

**(c) No production decision is frozen** — the seam observes only: it
forwards the original start, never blocks, refuses, reorders or delays a
start, and never touches a deadline.

**(d) The bounded drain, ONE overall bound** — `pending_starts() == 0` alone
is withdrawn as a proof (a start may follow the snapshot). The salvage of a
round is a bounded drain loop: `budget = 30.0` (one overall bound per
round), `deadline = now + budget`; then repeatedly — take `registry.snapshot()`
(the exact objects and their states) and append it to `attempts`; release the
gates of the round; `_salvage_threads(snapshot.owners, states=snapshot.hints,
seconds=10.0, total_seconds=max(0.0, deadline - now))`; stop when
`quiescent` (no pending start, no late start) **and** the salvage is proved,
or when `now >= deadline`. The last snapshot and the last salvage are the
recorded facts.

* The **30 s** is the bound of the **whole drain of that round**, not 30 s per
  resnapshot; the per-owner wait is cropped by the remaining overall budget
  and the budget is **never reset** between the rounds of the loop nor between
  the rounds of the test.
* The quiescence assertion comes **after** the drain, never before it: an
  unquiet round is a **FAIL/UNKNOWN** — the gates are still released and
  **all** the registered owners are still attempted; the drain repeats
  boundedly; if the bound is reached the proof fails truthfully (`'the owners
  of the round were not quiescent'`) with the last snapshot and the last
  salvage recorded.
* The drain never renews a deadline of the product and never claims that the
  product released a resource.

**(e) The producers** are the ones the product starts: pump and heartbeat
(`C:1772-1783`), closer (`C:1530-1534`), channel drain (`B:5256-5278`), the
two endpoint workers (`B:4683-4702`) — all observed, because the seam
observes every `threading.Thread.start` of the armed window.

### 10.4 (D4) — the exact close failure, the exact unwinding order

**(a) The substring test is withdrawn.** The oracle is exact: exactly one
recorded failure; `owner == 'controlled:transport'`; `type == 'RuntimeError'`
(the exact class); `detail == 'controlled_close_failure'` (the exact
message); and no record with another owner or another type. In **every**
checkpoint `child_close_failures` (`T10:2929-2964`) must be **empty**: an
unrelated close or endpoint error fails the subcase instead of being
tolerated next to the intended failure.

**(b) The exact ordering** for the four checkpoints whose deliberate failure
is raised by the fixture: (1) the deliberate failure is **raised** inside the
region (sentinel / ordinary assertion / constructor seam); (2) the region
runs **every** cleanup action in a fixed order — the gates → the close of the
transport → the close of each endpoint → `_capture_stop` → the owner
enumeration → the drain + salvage → the proof → the document; (3) the region
**records** every additional error and raises `AssertionError(proof)` **only**
when no original failure is unwinding; (4) the original failure leaves the
region and is caught by the outer `except`, **after every other cleanup
action has been attempted**; (5) the outer `except` records the exact type
and the exact token.

`CLOSE_FAILURE` is the single case whose failure is produced **by the
cleanup itself**: the failing close is **recorded** with its exact type and
exact message and the region **continues** with every other cleanup action —
it is therefore **not** an unwinding case, and the design says so instead of
claiming it. No expected sentinel is caught before the cleanup region: the
outer `except` is the only catch and it lies outside the region.

**(c) The original failure is preserved** — an error met by the region while
an original failure unwinds leaves the **original** re-raised, the additional
error recorded in the document and the overall proof failed; the test never
reports a successful cleanup on an error.

### 10.5 (D5) — every cleanup error is preserved

**(a) The classification is inside the per-owner guard** (it was outside the
try of the salvage in §4.5). Per owner: `state = None; errors = []`; then
`_owner_start_state(...)` inside its own `try` (a failure ⇒ an `errors`
entry of kind `STATE` and `state = 'UNKNOWN'`); if the state is not
`STARTED` the owner is recorded with `alive=None, joined=None` and its
errors; otherwise `thread.join(wait)` in its own `try` (kind `JOIN`) and
`bool(thread.is_alive())` in its own `try` (kind `LIVENESS`, `alive = None`
on a failure); the fact of the owner keeps `alive`, `joined` and the **list**
`errors`.

* **every** owner is attempted — a failure of the classification, of the join
  or of the liveness query never aborts the iteration;
* every error is retained in a **list** with its kind, exact type and detail
  — never a single "first error", never a silent `UNKNOWN` without a
  diagnostic; an owner whose classification failed is `UNKNOWN` **with** the
  diagnostic and it fails the proof, which names the owner and the error;
* the display form (the thread name) and the identity (`id(thread)`) are
  separate fields — the identity is never read out of a display string.

**(b) `KeyboardInterrupt` / `SystemExit`** — the interruption is recorded
with its identity; with no original unwinding it is re-raised at the end of
the region; with an original unwinding the **original** is preserved and
re-raised and the interruption is an additional recorded error. The region
never reports a successful cleanup when an interruption occurred.

**(c) `_capture_stop` reads the real facts** — the **real** keys of the
evidence DTO (`C:2343-2444`), among them `background_threads`, which **is** a
valid key (`C:2399-2406`); the object identities are kept apart from the
display JSON. The capture holds: `returned` (the actual dict of
`session.stop()`), `exception` (`{'type','detail'}`), `live_state`
(`session.state`), `evidence` (the full dict of `session.evidence()`),
`evidence_state`, `evidence_broker_retained`, `evidence_failures`,
`evidence_background_threads`, `evidence_closer_start_state` (the real keys
of that dict), `retained_broker_is_session_broker` (a bool: the identity),
`retained_broker_id` (`id(session.broker)`: the identity) and `snapshot_error`
(`{'type','detail'}` when the snapshot failed).

* the identities are **not** read out of the display JSON — they are the live
  facts of the session; the display JSON is kept as the DTO it is;
* a valid key is never read through a `.get()` that could hide an absent
  value — a missing value is a fact to be asserted;
* if the snapshot raises, `snapshot_error` carries the exact type and detail
  and the capture is **not** silently empty;
* the capture is taken **before** the salvage and on **both** paths.

### 10.6 (D2, D6) — the state transitions after the corrections

| from | event | to | durable document |
|---|---|---|---|
| `PLANNED` | the operation is entered (`attempt()` **before** the construction, the phase body, the barrier or the stop) | `ATTEMPTED` | none yet |
| `PLANNED` | the end of the test, never entered | `NOT_RUN` | `{'status': 'NOT_RUN', 'reason': <type of the first failure>}` — no fact of the phase |
| `ATTEMPTED` | the raw facts are written, before the assertions | `ATTEMPTED` | `{'status': 'EXECUTED', ...raw facts...}` |
| `ATTEMPTED` | every check of the operation held | `PASSED` | the **check** document — terminal |
| `ATTEMPTED` | an assertion raised or the operation itself raised | `FAIL` | the check document: `{'status': 'FAIL', 'executed': True, 'reason': <type>, 'detail': <str>, ...raw facts...}` |
| `ATTEMPTED` | the phase reached its end | `PASSED` / `FAIL` | the **result** document, written **last**; PASSED only when the barrier check, both stop checks **and** the cleanup passed; a later failed stop ⇒ FAIL while the earlier checks keep PASSED |
| `PASSED` / `FAIL` / `NOT_RUN` | anything | unchanged (**terminal**) | never overwritten by the inventory |

The **result** and the **check** documents are different: the result carries
the overall outcome, the checks carry their own facts; a failed check never
rewrites a passed check and the inventory never rewrites either.
