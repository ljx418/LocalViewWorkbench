# N1 correction 11: the mapping of every residual sub-path

Every residual of the frozen review of the correction 10 is mapped here to the
**actual production path**, to the **trigger** that drives it, to the
**oracle** that the product has to satisfy, to the **negative** case and its
exact boundary, to the **returned facts** and to the **salvage** of the owner
of the test.  The last column names the frozen node that carries the proof.

The dynamic status of every line stays the one of the isolated pre review of the
correction 10.  This document closes **no** Major and it signs **no** Stage.
